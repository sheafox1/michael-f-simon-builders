Our Story

Four generations of Simon builders

Michael Simon started building farm buildings in Waunakee in 1893, working for a dollar a day. His son Michael F. took over in 1952 and incorporated Michael F. Simon Builders in 1967. Today Paul Simon, the fourth generation, is personally involved with each customer.
